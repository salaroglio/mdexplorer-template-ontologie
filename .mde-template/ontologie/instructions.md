## Ontologies

- `ontology/` is the group's ontology: domain, competency questions, schema, rules, examples, queries and check.
  When you are asked about the ontology, start from `ontology/questions.md`: it says what the ontology is for.
- The round is the workflow `.mde-template/ontologie/workflows/ontology.md`: do not start its agents yourself and do not
  write their files in their place.
- To know whether a Turtle file can be read, whether the data respect the rules or what a query answers, use the tool
  `rdf_check`: do not guess.
- The method's theory, if the project asked for it, is in `theory/ontologie/`: use it to explain how to model, how to
  query and what Fuseki brings. If it is missing and would help, offer to download it (tool `add_theory`, id
  `ontologie`) and download it only after the person says yes.
- The files in `.mde-template/ontologie/` belong to the method and arrive with its updates: do not modify them.
