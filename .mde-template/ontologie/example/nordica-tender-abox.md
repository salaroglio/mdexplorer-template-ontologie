# ABox — nordica-tender

## TL;DR

The instances of the example tender, taken from `ontology/sources/nordica-tender.md`: the contracting authority Nordica
Crediti, five calls, Pentagroup's five criteria and the four roles that evaluate them. 68 triples, all conforming to
the rules.

- Three open calls with their deadline, two closed and already in the register.
- Criteria 1 and 5 block; 2 is evaluated by the technical lead, 3 by the delivery lead, 4 by the legal lead.
- All the data are invented.
