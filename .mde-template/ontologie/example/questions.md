# The competency questions

## TL;DR

The five questions the tender ontology must be able to answer, each with its expected answer according to
`ontology/sources/nordica-tender.md`. They are the acceptance tests: the ontology is finished when all of them have the
expected answer.

- Three questions on the calls (which are open, a deadline, the register).
- Two on the criteria (which block, who evaluates competence).
- Offers and people are outside: no question asks for them.

## The questions

| Id | Question | Answer | Expected answer | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| c1 | Which calls are open? | list | NC-2027-011, NC-2027-012, NC-2027-014 | §1 |
| c2 | What is the offer deadline of the call NC-2027-014? | date | 2027-04-30 | §1 |
| c3 | Is the call NC-2026-009 already in the register? | yes/no | yes | §1 |
| c4 | Which criteria block the evaluation of a call? | list | 1 Sector and nature, 5 Time to respond | §2 |
| c5 | Who evaluates the competence criterion? | list | technical lead | §2 |

## Outside the questions

The offers, the company's people, the leads' sheets: the domain names them but no question asks for them.
