# The domain

## TL;DR

The example ontology models a contracting authority's calls for tenders and the criteria a company uses to decide
whether to evaluate them: just enough to answer the questions of the tender round. It starts from a single, invented
document.

- Inside: calls, contracting authority, criteria, who evaluates the criteria; outside: offers, people, sheets.
- One starting document: `ontology/sources/nordica-tender.md`.
- Prefix `gara:`, base address `https://example.org/ontology/gara#`.

## What is modelled

A contracting authority's calls for tenders and the criteria Pentagroup uses to decide whether a call deserves an evaluation.

## Inside and outside

Inside: the calls (code, title, status, deadline, whether they are already in the register), the contracting authority,
the five criteria (whether they block, who evaluates them). Outside: the offers, the people, the leads' sheets, the
contents of the specifications.

## The documents to start from

- `ontology/sources/nordica-tender.md` — the calls of Nordica Crediti and the criteria of Pentagroup.
