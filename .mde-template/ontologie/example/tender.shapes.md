# Shapes — tender

## TL;DR

The rules the tender data respect: a call has exactly one code in the form NC-YYYY-NNN, a status open or closed, at
most one deadline that is a date, and its contracting authority; a criterion has a number from 1 to 5 and someone who
evaluates it.

- Two shapes: `gara:BandoShape` and `gara:CriterioShape`.
- Every rule has its message in English.
- With the example data: no violation (`ontology/verification.md`).
