# The Nordica Crediti tender — the facts

## TL;DR

The facts the example ontology starts from: the calls of Nordica Crediti on 8 March 2027, the five criteria Pentagroup
uses to evaluate a call and who evaluates each one. They are taken from the tender of the MdExplorer demo; all the data
are **invented**.

- Five calls: three open, two closed and already in Pentagroup's register.
- Five criteria: 1 and 5 block, the other three are weighed together.
- Every criterion has someone who evaluates it: account manager, technical lead, legal lead, delivery lead.

## The calls (§1)

| Code | Title | Status | Offer deadline | In the register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NC-2027-014 | Master platform for the enforced collection of local authorities' taxes (ATLANTE project) | open | 30/04/2027 | no |
| NC-2027-012 | Supply of software licences for document management | open | 24/03/2027 | no |
| NC-2027-011 | Ordinary and extraordinary maintenance of the buildings in the portfolio | open | 20/04/2027 | no |
| NC-2026-021 | Payment portal for debtors | closed | — | yes |
| NC-2026-009 | Contact centre service for out-of-court recovery | closed | — | yes |

The contracting authority of all of them is **Nordica Crediti S.p.A.**

## The criteria (§2)

| Number | Criterion | Blocks | Who evaluates it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sector and nature | yes | account manager |
| 2 | Competence | no | technical lead |
| 3 | Feasibility of the timeline | no | delivery lead |
| 4 | Contractual risk | no | legal lead |
| 5 | Time to respond (at least 21 days) | yes | account manager |
